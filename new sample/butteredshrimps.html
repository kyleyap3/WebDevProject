Garlic Buttered Shrimp

Filipino buttered shrimp (often halabos na hipon or garlic butter shrimp) is a quick, savory dish featuring fresh shrimp sautéed in abundant butter, garlic, and sometimes lemon-lime soda (Sprite/7-Up) or oyster sauce for a sweet-savory glaze. Best cooked with heads on for flavor, it is served hot with rice.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs cleaned shrimp
  • 2 tablespoons chopped parsley
  • 1/4 cup butter
  • 1 head crushed garlic
  • 1 cup lemon lime soda
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice
  • salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Marinate the shrimp in lemon soda for about 10 minutes.
  2. Melt the butter in a pan.
  3. Add the garlic. Cook in low heat until the color turns light brown
  4. Put-in the shrimp. Adjust heat to high. Stir-fry until shrimp turns orange.
  5. Season with ground black pepper, salt, and lemon juice. Stir.
  6. Add parsley. Cook for 30 seconds.
  7. Serve hot.
